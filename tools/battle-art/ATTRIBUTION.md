# Battle screen artwork

Everything the battle screen draws is generated by `build_battle_art.py` from the sources
below. Nothing here was hand-edited after generation -- re-running the script reproduces
`app/src/main/assets/battle/*` and `app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/battle_bg_graveyard.png`
byte for byte.

## Monsters -- `source/monsters/*.png`

Dungeon Crawl Stone Soup tiles, taken from the **CC0** export at
<https://github.com/crawl/tiles> (`releases/Nov-2015/mon/**`). That repository exists precisely
to separate the tiles whose artists released their copyright from the ones with unclear
ownership; only files from the CC0 export are vendored here, and none of them appear in that
repository's `TILES_UNDER_UNKNOWN_LICENSE.md`.

CC0 means no attribution is required. This file is here anyway, because knowing where a sprite
came from is worth more than the licence obliges.

## Heroes -- `hero.png`, `hero_mage.png`, `hero_archer.png`

One page per school. All three are cut to the same four clips the arena plays -- idle, attack,
hurt, cast -- and each page carries its own frame size and feet line, because the three packs are
drawn at different resolutions and are deliberately not resampled to a common grid.

### Chiến binh -- `hero.png`

Re-cut from `TurnBasedBattle/Assets/_Project/Sprites/Characters/HeroKnight.png`: the free
"Hero Knight - Pixel Art" pack that the sibling Unity project already ships. The frame runs and
their frame rates are read out of that project's own `.anim` files, so the fight plays the same
idle, swing, flinch and guard the Unity game plays.

### Pháp sư -- `hero_mage.png`

**Wizard Pack** by LuizMelo (<https://luizmelo.itch.io/wizard-pack>), **CC0**: free for
commercial use, attribution not required. Source strips in `source/heroes/wizard/`.

Halved on the way in. The pack draws the wizard 86px tall against the knight's 40 and the
huntress's 36; at native size he would loom over both. An integer 1/2 is the only reduction that
keeps pixel art crisp, and it lands him at 43px -- between the other two, with the pixel density
matched as well.

`CAST` is the pack's second attack; there is no "holding a spell" animation to borrow.

### Xạ thủ -- `hero_archer.png`

**Huntress 2** by LuizMelo (<https://luizmelo.itch.io/huntress-2>), **CC0**, licence text kept at
`source/heroes/huntress/License.txt`. Source strips in `source/heroes/huntress/`. Used at native
size.

This pack animates a single attack, so `CAST` repeats it: drawing a bow is what casting looks
like for an archer too.

`arrow.png` is the pack's own arrow (`Sprites/Arrow/Static.png`, 24x5), copied across unchanged.
The archer and the mage do not close the distance to strike -- they stand and send something --
so the arena needed a projectile to draw. The mage's bolt has no sprite: the wizard pack animates
its spells inside the attack frames, so the arena draws a circle of light instead.

## Backdrop -- `battle_bg_graveyard.png`

The graveyard frame of the free "2D Cartoon Parallax Background" pack, likewise already in
`TurnBasedBattle/Assets/ThirdParty/`, centre-cropped from 4320x2160 to a stage-shaped 1440x900.

Both of these are Unity Asset Store packs the sibling project ships under their free licences.
They are re-used here rather than re-downloaded so the two projects stay visually identical --
which is the whole point of this screen's redesign.
